## Question-bank content

The repository ships **only a fictional demo bank** generated by
`scripts/make-demo-bank.cjs` (original example questions written for this template,
plus a programmatically drawn placeholder figure). No third-party exam questions,
book scans, or publisher content is included or redistributed.

If you import your own questions, images, or explanations, **you** are responsible
for holding the necessary rights. Do not assume any published question bank may be
redistributed. Remove or replace the demo bank before publishing if you add
content you are unsure about.
